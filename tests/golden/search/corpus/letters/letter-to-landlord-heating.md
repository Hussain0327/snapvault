# Letter to Landlord - Heating Repair Request

Dear Mr. Alvarez,

I am writing about the heating system in apartment 4B at 218 Birchwood Lane, which has not worked since the evening of January 14.
The thermostat reads 58 degrees Fahrenheit most mornings, well below the 68 degrees required under section 7 of the lease.

I called the emergency maintenance line on January 15 and again on January 17, but no technician has visited the unit yet.
My two children are in the apartment during the day, and the cold has already led to one of them missing school with a cough.

Under the terms of our lease, repairs affecting habitability are supposed to be addressed within 72 hours of notice.
It has now been eight days since my first call.
I am asking that a technician be sent no later than January 24, or that a portable heater be provided in the meantime at no cost to us.

If the repair cannot be scheduled by that date, I will need to look into the tenant remedies available under the local housing code, including potential rent withholding until the heat is restored.
I would much rather resolve this directly with you, since we have had a good relationship over the two years we have lived here.

Please let me know when a technician can come by.
I am generally home after 4 PM on weekdays and all day on weekends.

Sincerely,
Renata Osei
Apartment 4B, 218 Birchwood Lane
