# Congratulations Letter for a Graduation

Dear Noor,

Congratulations on graduating from Ashcombe University with your degree in mechanical engineering.
Watching you go from struggling with your first physics course four years ago to walking across that stage on May 17 has been one of the proudest things I have gotten to witness.

I still remember you calling me, frustrated, during your sophomore year after failing a thermodynamics exam.
You talked about switching majors that same week.
I am glad you stuck with it, because the senior project you told me about, the one redesigning a bicycle gear system for lower manufacturing cost, sounds like exactly the kind of work you were meant to do.

Your parents mentioned you already have an offer from Draymont Engineering starting in July, with a starting salary they seemed genuinely thrilled about.
That is a wonderful way to start this next chapter, and it says a lot about how far you have come since that thermodynamics exam.

I am sending a small gift separately, a set of drafting tools that belonged to your grandfather.
I think he would have liked knowing they ended up with an engineer in the family.

Enjoy the summer before the new job starts.
You have earned every bit of this.

With so much pride,
Aunt Halima
