# Thank You Letter to a Mentor

Dear Professor Klein,

I wanted to write before the new job takes over my schedule completely.
I start as a data analyst at Ferrow Logistics on April 6, and I do not think I would have gotten there without the two years I spent as your research assistant.

I still remember how patient you were the first semester, when I could barely read a regression output without help.
By the time I left the lab in December, I was running the analysis for two of your grant reports largely on my own, which felt impossible back in that first meeting.

The recommendation letter you wrote in February clearly made a difference; the hiring manager mentioned it by name during my final interview and said it was the most specific reference letter she had read all year.
I know how much time that takes to write well, and I do not take it for granted.

If you are ever looking for someone to help with the introductory statistics course again, I would be glad to come back as a guest instructor for a session or two.
It would be a small way of paying forward everything I learned in your lab.

Thank you again for everything, Professor Klein.
I hope our paths cross again soon.

Warm regards,
Aditi Rao
