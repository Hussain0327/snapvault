# Letter Disputing an Insurance Claim Decision

To the Claims Review Department,

I am writing to dispute the denial of claim number HX-88214-B, dated February 20, regarding water damage to the basement of my home at 55 Larkspur Avenue.
The denial letter states the damage resulted from "gradual seepage," which is excluded under my policy, but I believe this determination is incorrect.

The damage occurred over a single 24-hour period following a burst pipe on February 11, not through gradual seepage.
I have attached a plumber's invoice, dated February 12, that explicitly describes the cause as "a sudden rupture in a supply line," along with photographs taken the morning the damage was discovered.

My policy, number PL-4471902, includes coverage for sudden and accidental water damage up to 25,000 dollars, minus my 1,000 dollar deductible.
The contractor's repair estimate for the flooring and drywall alone comes to 6,400 dollars, well within that coverage limit.

I am requesting that the claim be reopened and reviewed by a different adjuster, given the clear evidence that this was a sudden event rather than gradual seepage.
Please let me know within 15 business days, as outlined in the policy's dispute resolution section, whether the claim will be reconsidered.

I can be reached at the number on file if any additional documentation is needed.

Sincerely,
Farrukh Islam
Policy PL-4471902
