# Letter to Landlord - Water Leak Report

Dear Ms. Chen,

I am writing to report an ongoing water leak in unit 12 at 940 Sycamore Court.
Water has been dripping from the ceiling above the kitchen sink since Tuesday, March 3, and has already warped a section of the laminate flooring near the cabinet.

I left a voicemail with the property management office on March 3 and sent a follow-up email on March 5 with photos attached, but I have not received a response from either.
The dripping has slowed since I placed a bucket under it, but the ceiling is now visibly discolored in a patch about 30 centimeters across.

Our lease states in section 9 that the property manager is responsible for structural repairs, including plumbing above the unit, within five business days of notice.
That deadline passed on March 10.
I estimate the flooring damage alone could cost several hundred dollars to repair if the leak is not stopped soon, and I would rather we address it now than argue about the deposit later.

Could you send a plumber this week?
I am available for access any weekday between 8 AM and 6 PM, and my neighbor in unit 11 has a key if I am not home.

I have kept copies of both the voicemail log and the emailed photos in case they are needed.

Thank you,
Owen Marsh
Unit 12, 940 Sycamore Court
