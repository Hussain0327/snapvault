# Herb Roast Chicken

A Sunday staple in this house, adapted from a recipe card that belonged to my grandmother.
Simple, forgiving, and good with almost any side dish.

## Ingredients

- 1 whole chicken, about 1.8 kilograms
- 4 tablespoons softened butter
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh rosemary
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh thyme
- Zest of 1 lemon
- Salt and black pepper
- 1 yellow onion, quartered

## Method

Pat the chicken completely dry with paper towels; a dry skin crisps up much better than a damp one.
Rub the skin with softened butter mixed with chopped rosemary, thyme, and lemon zest before roasting.
Season generously inside the cavity and under the skin with salt and pepper.
Place the quartered onion in the cavity to add flavor to the pan drippings.

Roast the 1.8 kilogram chicken at 200 degrees Celsius for 75 minutes, basting once at the halfway point.
Check doneness with a thermometer inserted into the thickest part of the thigh; it should read 74 degrees Celsius.
Let the chicken rest for 15 minutes under foil before carving so the juices redistribute through the meat.

## Serving Notes

Serve with the pan drippings spooned over the sliced breast for extra moisture and flavor.
Leftover chicken keeps well in the refrigerator for up to four days in a sealed container.
The carcass makes an excellent base for stock; simmer it with the onion skins and a bay leaf for two hours.

## Variations

Swap the rosemary and thyme for tarragon and chives when tarragon is in season.
A splash of white wine added to the roasting pan halfway through deepens the flavor of the drippings considerably.
