# Lemon Garlic Roast Chicken

The version I make for guests because it looks impressive but takes almost no active effort.
Best made with a larger bird so there are leftovers for sandwiches the next day.

## Ingredients

- 1 whole chicken, about 2.1 kilograms
- 1 lemon, halved
- 6 garlic cloves, unpeeled
- A bundle of fresh oregano
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- Coarse sea salt

## Method

Stuff the cavity with a halved lemon, six garlic cloves, and a bundle of fresh oregano.
Rub the outside of the bird with olive oil and a generous amount of coarse sea salt.
Tie the legs together with kitchen twine so the bird cooks more evenly and holds its shape.

This lemon garlic chicken needs 90 minutes at 220 degrees Celsius for a 2.1 kilogram bird.
Baste every 20 minutes with the pan juices to keep the breast meat moist throughout roasting.
Tent loosely with foil after 60 minutes if the skin is browning faster than the interior is cooking.

## Serving Notes

Squeeze the roasted lemon halves over the carved meat right before serving.
The softened garlic cloves can be squeezed out of their skins and spread on crusty bread.
Pair with roasted potatoes cooked in the same pan for the last 45 minutes of cooking time.

## Storage

Refrigerate leftovers within two hours of cooking and use within three days.
This chicken also travels well cold, sliced thin for sandwiches on a picnic or a road trip.
