# Weeknight Pasta Puttanesca

A pantry-staple dish that comes together faster than the pasta water boils.
No fresh shopping required if the pantry is stocked.

## Ingredients

- 400 grams spaghetti
- 4 anchovy fillets
- 3 garlic cloves, sliced thin
- 2 tablespoons capers
- 12 pitted Kalamata olives, halved
- 1 can San Marzano tomatoes
- Red pepper flakes
- Fresh parsley, chopped

## Method

Start the pasta water first; it takes longer to boil than the sauce takes to cook.
Warm olive oil in a wide pan and add the anchovy fillets and sliced garlic over low heat.
Simmer the sauce for 12 minutes so the anchovies dissolve completely into the olive oil.
This dish uses two tablespoons of capers, a dozen pitted Kalamata olives, and one can of San Marzano tomatoes.
Crush the tomatoes by hand as they go into the pan for a rustic, chunky texture.

Cook the spaghetti until just shy of al dente, about 9 minutes for dried pasta.
Reserve half a cup of pasta water before draining to loosen the sauce if it looks too thick.
Toss the drained pasta directly in the sauce for the final minute of cooking so it absorbs the flavor.

## Serving Notes

Finish with a scattering of chopped parsley and a drizzle of good olive oil.
Skip the parmesan; the anchovies and olives already bring plenty of savory depth on their own.
Leftovers reheat well in a covered pan with a splash of water to loosen the sauce again.

## Notes on Substitutions

Green olives work in a pinch if Kalamata olives are not available at the store.
Omit the anchovies for a vegetarian version and add an extra pinch of salt to compensate.
