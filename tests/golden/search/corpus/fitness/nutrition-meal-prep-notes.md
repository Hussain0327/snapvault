# Nutrition and Meal Prep Notes

Notes on the meal prep routine that supports the current training block, written up after a few months of trial and error.

## Weekly prep

Sunday is the main cooking day: about two hours to prepare five lunches and five dinners for the week.
Chicken thighs, rice, and roasted vegetables form the base of most lunches, since they reheat well without drying out.

## Macros

The current target is 160 grams of protein, 250 grams of carbohydrate, and 70 grams of fat per day, adjusted up on heavy training days.
Protein comes mostly from chicken, eggs, and a whey shake after workouts.

## Grocery list

A typical weekly shop costs around 85 dollars and includes 2 kilograms of chicken thighs, a dozen eggs, 3 kilograms of rice, and a rotating mix of broccoli, carrots, and spinach.
Buying chicken in bulk from the warehouse store cut the weekly grocery bill by about 15 dollars compared to the regular supermarket.

## Pre-workout

Eat a small meal 90 minutes before training: usually oatmeal with a banana and a scoop of protein powder.
Training on an empty stomach in January led to two dizzy spells during heavy squat sessions, so that experiment is over.

## Hydration

Aim for at least 3 liters of water a day, more on training days.
Electrolyte tablets get added to the water bottle on any day the workout runs past an hour.

## Supplements

Creatine monohydrate, 5 grams daily, has been part of the routine since December.
A multivitamin covers the gaps from a diet that is not always as varied as it should be, though whole food remains the priority over pills.
