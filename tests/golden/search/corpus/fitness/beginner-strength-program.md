# Beginner Strength Program

This is an 8-week, full-body strength program designed for someone new to barbell training.
Train three days a week with at least one rest day between sessions.

## Structure

Each session is built around three main lifts: squat, bench press, and deadlift or overhead press on alternating days.
Warm up with five minutes of light cardio and two easy sets of the first lift before loading up.

## Week 1-2

Work sets are 3 sets of 8 reps at a weight you could lift for 12.
Add 2.5 kg to the bar each session for squat and press, and 5 kg for deadlift, as long as every rep stays clean.

## Week 3-5

Move to 4 sets of 6 reps once the 8-rep weight feels easy for three sessions in a row.
Most beginners stall on bench press before squat or deadlift; if bench press stops progressing for two straight sessions, drop the weight by 10 percent and build back up.

## Week 6-8

Reps drop to 5 sets of 5, and the pace of adding weight slows to once per week rather than every session.
By week 8 most trainees are squatting around 1.5 times bodyweight for a set of 5.

## Before you start

Anyone with a prior back or knee injury should see a physician before starting this program, and should ask specifically about clearance for loaded squats and deadlifts.

## Recovery

Sleep at least seven hours a night during this program; the strength gains in week 6-8 depend on recovery as much as on the lifting itself.
Protein intake around 1.6 grams per kilogram of bodyweight per day supports the added training volume.

## Equipment

A power rack with safety pins is required for the squat and bench press days.
Lifting without safety pins on a failed rep is the most common cause of gym injuries reported by our coaching staff last year.
