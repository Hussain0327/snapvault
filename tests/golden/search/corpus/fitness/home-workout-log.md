# Home Workout Log

A running log of home training sessions, kept since switching from the gym to a small apartment setup with adjustable dumbbells and a pull-up bar.

## February 2026

Feb 2: Full body, 45 minutes.
Dumbbell squats 3x12 at 24 kg total, push-ups 3x15, dumbbell rows 3x10 at 20 kg per hand.
Felt strong; sleep was good the night before.

Feb 9: Pull-up day.
Hit 8 consecutive pull-ups for the first time, up from 5 at the start of January.
Added a chin-up set afterward to keep volume up since pull-ups are still limited by grip strength.

Feb 16: Skipped session, sick with a cold.
Rested instead of pushing through it.

Feb 23: Full body, 50 minutes.
New personal record on dumbbell squats: 28 kg total for a set of 10.
Push-ups dropped to 3x12, likely from the missed session earlier in the month.

## March 2026

Mar 2: Added a resistance band for face pulls, 3x15, to address the shoulder tightness that showed up during pull-up day last month.

Mar 9: Pull-up day.
10 consecutive pull-ups, a new best.
Grip strength no longer feels like the limiting factor; shoulder fatigue is now the main constraint late in the set.

Mar 16: Full body, 55 minutes.
Dumbbell squats at 30 kg total for 8 reps.
Considering buying a heavier dumbbell set since the current pair tops out at 20 kg per hand.

## Notes

The adjustable dumbbells top out at 20 kg per hand, which is starting to limit progress on rows and presses.
Planning to add resistance bands for the upper-body pressing movements before investing in heavier weights.
