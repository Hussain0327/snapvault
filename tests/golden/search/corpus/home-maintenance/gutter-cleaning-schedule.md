# Gutter Cleaning Schedule

Notes on keeping the gutters clear, since a clogged gutter caused water damage to the fascia board two years ago.

## Schedule

Gutters are cleaned twice a year, in late October after leaf fall and again in early April before spring rains.
A quick visual check happens after any major storm, especially if wind brought down a lot of small branches.
The October cleaning is the more important of the two, since most of the leaf drop from the oak trees happens in a three-week window.

## Trouble Spots

The downspout on the northeast corner clogs most often because of an overhanging maple tree.
The section above the garage collects granules washed off the roof shingles and needs extra flushing with a hose.
A gutter guard was installed over the front porch section last year and has noticeably reduced debris there.

## Method

Cleaning starts by scooping out debris by hand, then flushing each section with a hose from the far end toward the downspout.
Downspouts are checked separately by running water through them to confirm nothing is blocking the pipe below ground level.
A stabilizer ladder is used on the two-story sections, since a standard extension ladder was judged too risky there.

## Cost

A professional gutter cleaning service charges $140 per visit, compared to about two hours of DIY labor.
Gutter guards for the remaining sections would cost an estimated $9 per linear foot if installed by a contractor.

## Next Steps

Consider extending the gutter guard installed over the porch to the northeast corner, since that section clogs the most.
Schedule the October cleaning for the first weekend after the oak trees finish dropping their leaves.
