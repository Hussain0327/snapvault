# Monthly Budget - August 2026

Personal budget tracking sheet, summarized from the checking account and credit card statements.

## Income

Total income for August was $5,400 after taxes, split between a $4,800 salary and $600 in freelance work.
The freelance payment arrived later than expected, on August 24th instead of the usual mid-month date.

## Fixed Expenses

Rent took the largest share of the August budget at $1,850, followed by $620 in groceries.
Utilities, including electricity, water, and internet, totaled $210 for the month.
Car insurance and a phone plan added another $190 in fixed monthly costs.

## Variable Expenses

Dining out came to $340 across the month, above the usual $250 target because of two birthday dinners.
Transportation costs, mostly gas and parking, totaled $130.
A one-time purchase of a new laptop charger added $45 to discretionary spending.

## Savings

The August budget left $410 unspent, which was transferred into the emergency fund at the end of the month.
An additional $300 automatic transfer to the retirement account happened on August 1st as scheduled, separate from the $410 surplus.

## Summary

Total expenses for August came to $3,290, against $5,400 in income, before counting the retirement transfer.
This is the third month in a row with a positive surplus after two lean months earlier in the year.

## Notes for Next Month

Dining out needs to come back down toward the $250 target since two more birthdays fall in September.
Consider moving the freelance income into a separate account so it does not get spent alongside the regular salary.
