# Monthly Budget - September 2026

Personal budget tracking sheet, summarized from the checking account and credit card statements.

## Income

Total income for September was $5,150 after taxes, all from salary since no freelance work came in.
The lack of freelance income this month was expected, since the client project wrapped up in August.

## Fixed Expenses

Rent stayed at $1,850, but a $340 car repair pushed transportation costs well above the usual budget.
Utilities came in slightly lower than August at $195, thanks to milder weather reducing air conditioning use.
Car insurance and the phone plan stayed flat at $190 combined.

## Variable Expenses

Dining out dropped back to $240 after two heavier months, close to the $250 target.
Groceries were $605, roughly in line with August's $620.
The car repair, an alternator replacement, was the single largest unplanned expense of the month.

## Savings

The September budget ran $95 over, covered by drawing down the emergency fund rather than a credit card.
No retirement transfer was skipped; the automatic $300 contribution still went through on September 1st as scheduled.

## Summary

Total expenses for September came to $3,420, against $5,150 in income, before counting the retirement transfer.
This breaks the three-month streak of positive surplus, mainly due to the unplanned car repair.

## Notes for Next Month

Rebuild the emergency fund withdrawal of $95 as soon as October income allows, before resuming any discretionary spending increases.
Get a quote for a car maintenance plan to avoid another surprise repair bill like this one.
