# Medical Expense Log - 2026

A running log of doctor visits and related costs, kept for tax records and insurance deductible tracking.

## March

The annual physical with Dr. Alvarez in March cost $180 after insurance, applied toward the annual deductible.
Bloodwork ordered during the same visit added another $65, billed separately by the lab.
Both charges were confirmed as applying to the $1,500 annual deductible on this plan.

## June

A visit to the physician for a sprained ankle in June resulted in a $95 co-pay plus $40 for the X-ray.
A follow-up visit two weeks later, to confirm the ankle had healed, added a smaller $30 co-pay.
No further imaging was needed at the follow-up, keeping that visit's cost lower than the first.

## July

A dermatologist visit for a routine skin check cost $150, since this specialist is out of network for the current plan.
The out-of-pocket maximum for this plan is $3,500 per year, and $1,240 has been spent so far by July.
Prescription costs for the month totaled $28 for two generic medications.

## August

A dental cleaning, covered separately under the dental plan, cost $0 out of pocket since it was the semiannual covered visit.
An urgent care visit for a minor burn cost $110, higher than a regular doctor visit but far less than an emergency room would have charged.

## Notes
