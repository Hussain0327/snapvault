# Winter Trip Packing Checklist

For the ten-day trip combining city stops with three days in the mountains.

## Clothing

Pack thermal base layers rated for temperatures down to negative 10 degrees Celsius for the mountain portion of the trip.
Bring one waterproof outer shell jacket and waterproof pants for the skiing days.
Pack two pairs of wool socks per day, since wet socks are the fastest way to get cold on the slopes.
A single set of dressier clothes covers the two dinners planned in the city before the mountain leg.

## Footwear

Waterproof winter boots are essential for the city stops, where sidewalks are often slushy after snow.
Ski boots are rented locally rather than packed, since checked ski boots add significant weight and bulk.

## Documents and Electronics

Bring a universal power adapter since European outlets use type C and F sockets, not the North American type A.
Print a paper copy of the hotel and rental car confirmations in case phone battery runs low in the cold.
Pack a portable battery pack, since cold weather drains phone batteries noticeably faster than usual.

## Baggage

Checked baggage is limited to 23 kilograms per bag on this airline before extra fees apply.
Ski equipment counts as a separate item with its own 30 kilogram limit and must be declared at booking.
A single carry-on backpack holds electronics, medication, and a change of clothes in case checked bags are delayed.

## Health and Comfort

Pack lip balm with SPF and moisturizer, since mountain air and indoor heating both dry out skin quickly.
Bring any prescription medication in original packaging along with a copy of the prescription for customs.

## Final Check

Confirm travel insurance covers winter sports, since some standard travel policies exclude skiing injuries by default.
