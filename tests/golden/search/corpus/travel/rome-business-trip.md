# Rome Business Trip Itinerary

Conference: European Systems Summit
Dates: March 9 to March 13, 2026

## Flights

The flight to Rome departs on March 9th at 7:45 AM and connects through Frankfurt with a 90-minute layover.
The return flight leaves Rome at 8:15 PM on March 13th and arrives home the next morning.
Both segments are booked in economy through the corporate travel portal under booking reference RM-88214.

## Lodging

The hotel, a Marriott near the conference center, costs 210 euros per night for the four-night stay.
Check-in is at 3:00 PM on the 9th and check-out is at 11:00 AM on the 13th.
The hotel offers a conference rate that includes breakfast, which is not itemized separately on the bill.

## Ground Transport

A pre-booked airport transfer picks up at the arrivals hall for 45 euros each way, cheaper than a metered taxi.
The conference center is a 10-minute walk from the hotel, so no local transit pass is needed for this trip.

## Conference Schedule

The keynote runs from 9:00 AM to 10:30 AM on March 10th, followed by breakout sessions through the afternoon.
A team dinner with the Milan office is planned for the evening of March 11th at a restaurant near the Pantheon.
The last conference session ends at noon on March 13th, leaving the afternoon free before the evening flight.

## Expenses

Per diem for this trip is capped at 65 euros per day for meals, submitted with receipts within two weeks of return.
The hotel and flights are billed directly to the corporate card, so only meals and incidentals need to be expensed separately.
Any taxi rides beyond the airport transfer should be logged with the destination and business purpose noted.

## Packing Reminders

Bring the badge and printed itinerary for the conference registration desk, since digital check-in has been unreliable at past events.
Pack one business suit and one backup shirt in case the dinner runs into a second evening event.
