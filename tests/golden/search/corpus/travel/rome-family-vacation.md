# Rome Family Vacation Itinerary

Trip: Summer vacation with the kids
Dates: July 18 to July 25, 2026

## Flights

The family flies to Rome on July 18th on a direct overnight flight landing at 6:20 AM local time.
The return flight departs Rome at 11:40 AM on July 25th, direct back home.
Seats were selected in advance so the kids sit next to at least one adult on both legs.

## Lodging

We booked a three-bedroom apartment near Trastevere for 180 euros per night for the seven-night stay.
The building has an elevator and air conditioning, both confirmed with the host in advance since we are traveling in July heat.
Check-in is self-service with a lockbox code sent the day before arrival.

## Sightseeing

Skip-the-line tickets for the Colosseum and Roman Forum cost 24 euros per adult and 12 euros per child.
A half-day tour of the Vatican Museums and Sistine Chapel is booked for the morning of July 20th, before the afternoon heat.
Gelato breaks are planned after every major sight, mostly to keep the kids motivated through the walking.

## Daily Plan

Mornings are reserved for major sights before the midday heat, with pool time or a rest at the apartment in the afternoon.
July 22nd is a day trip to the beach town of Ostia, about 45 minutes from the apartment by regional train.
The last full day, July 24th, is left unplanned as a buffer in case anyone needs a slower pace.

## Budget

Total trip budget, excluding flights, is set at 3,200 euros for lodging, food, activities, and local transport.
A children's transit pass covers unlimited metro and bus rides for the week at a fraction of the adult fare.

## Packing Reminders

Pack sunscreen, hats, and a refillable water bottle for each family member given the July heat.
Bring copies of passports separate from the originals in case anything is lost during the trip.
