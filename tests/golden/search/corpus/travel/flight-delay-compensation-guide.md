# Flight Delay and Denied Boarding Compensation Notes

Personal reference notes gathered after a delayed flight, for use on future trips.

## EU261 Basics

Under EU261, a flight delay of three hours or more on a European carrier can entitle passengers to up to 600 euros in compensation.
The rule applies to flights departing from an EU airport on any airline, and to flights arriving in the EU on an EU-based airline.
Compensation amount depends on the flight distance: 250 euros for short flights, 400 euros for medium flights, and 600 euros for long flights.

## Extraordinary Circumstances

Airlines are not required to pay compensation when the delay is caused by extraordinary circumstances such as severe weather.
Air traffic control strikes and security threats also typically count as extraordinary circumstances under the regulation.
Technical faults with the aircraft, however, are usually not considered extraordinary and can still qualify for compensation.

## Filing a Claim

Claims must be filed within the statute of limitations, which varies by country but is often two to six years.
The airline's own claim form is the first step, though many travelers use a claims agency instead for a percentage of the payout.
Keep the boarding pass, any delay notifications, and receipts for meals or lodging purchased because of the delay.

## Denied Boarding

Denied boarding due to overbooking carries similar compensation rules to long delays, plus the airline must offer a choice of rerouting or refund.
Volunteers who give up their seat voluntarily negotiate their own compensation, which is often more generous than the mandatory minimum.

## US Domestic Flights

US regulations do not guarantee cash compensation for delays the way EU261 does, though denied boarding compensation rules do apply domestically.
Airlines publish their own delay and cancellation policies, which are worth checking before booking a tight connection.

## Practical Tips

Photograph the departure board showing the delay, since airlines sometimes dispute how long a delay actually lasted.
