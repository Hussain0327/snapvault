# Hiring Committee Notes - Senior Backend Engineer

Date: August 12, 2026
Attendees: Sam Okafor (hiring manager), two technical panel members, one HR partner

## Purpose

Decide on an offer for the senior backend engineer role after four candidates completed onsite interviews.

## Decisions

The committee voted 4 to 1 to extend an offer to the senior backend candidate at a base salary of $148,000.
Two candidates were rejected after the technical panel found their system design answers too shallow for the seniority level.
A third candidate was placed on hold pending a decision on a separate, more junior opening that might be a better fit.
The committee agreed the offer should include a signing bonus of $10,000 to match a competing offer the candidate mentioned.

## Action Items

- Sam Okafor will call the top candidate before Friday to confirm the start date and answer any remaining questions.
- The HR partner will draft the formal offer letter, including the signing bonus, by Wednesday.
- The technical panel will document interview notes for the two rejected candidates in case either reapplies later.

## Discussion

One panel member dissented on the offer, arguing the candidate's testing experience was thinner than the team needed, but was outvoted after the hiring manager noted the candidate's strong systems background.
The committee discussed whether to also make an offer to the third candidate immediately, but decided to wait until the junior role is finalized.
Sam Okafor noted the team has budget for exactly one senior hire this quarter, which limits how many offers can go out.

## Follow Up

If the top candidate declines, the committee will reconvene within a week to discuss next steps rather than restarting the search from scratch.
