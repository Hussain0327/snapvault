# Office Relocation Planning Meeting

Date: September 3, 2026
Attendees: Facilities lead, IT lead, two department heads

## Purpose

Plan the move out of the current office and select a new location before the lease expires.

## Decisions

The lease at the current Market Street office expires on December 31st, forcing a move by early January.
The committee shortlisted two buildings, one in the Financial District and one in SoMa, both under $9,200 per month.
The group voted to prioritize the SoMa building because it offers more desks for the same monthly rent.
Moving day was tentatively set for January 9th, a Friday, to minimize disruption to the work week.

## Action Items

- IT will need three weeks of lead time to migrate the server room equipment before the physical move.
- The facilities lead will negotiate the SoMa lease terms and aim to sign by October 1st.
- Each department head will submit desk and headcount requirements by September 20th.

## Discussion

The IT lead raised concerns that a January move date leaves little buffer if the server room migration runs long, since the current lease has no grace period after December 31st.
One department head asked whether a shorter-term sublet could bridge any gap, and facilities agreed to explore that as a backup option.
The other building option, in the Financial District, was ruled out mainly because it lacked a loading dock large enough for server racks.

## Budget

The move itself, including movers and IT downtime, is budgeted at $22,000, separate from the new lease cost.
Facilities will track actual moving costs against this budget and report back at the October all-hands.
