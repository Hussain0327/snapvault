# Q4 Budget Review

Date: October 21, 2026
Attendees: Elena Rodriguez (Finance), Priya Nair (Engineering), Marcus Webb (Product)

## Purpose

Set the Q4 budget in light of Q3 actuals and the upcoming holiday marketing push.

## Decisions

The team approved a Q4 marketing budget increase from $62,000 to $95,000 to support the holiday campaign.
Finance denied the request for an additional $20,000 in contractor budget, citing the Q3 overspend on the migration project.
The engineering hardware refresh deferred from Q3 was approved for Q4 at a capped total of $48,000.
Elena Rodriguez confirmed the overall Q4 department budget ceiling stays at $340,000, up slightly from Q3.

## Action Items

- Elena Rodriguez will present the Q4 forecast to leadership on November 12th.
- Marcus Webb will finalize the holiday campaign creative brief by November 1st so the marketing spend can start on schedule.
- Priya Nair will source hardware quotes from two vendors before placing the $48,000 hardware order.

## Discussion

Marcus Webb argued that the holiday campaign needed the full $95,000 to match last year's reach, and the committee agreed after reviewing last year's return on ad spend.
Priya Nair flagged that the denied contractor budget means the search indexing project will slip by roughly three weeks.
Elena Rodriguez said the overspend in Q3 was the main reason finance wants tighter contractor tracking going into Q4.

## Next Meeting

The next budget review is scheduled for January 20th, once Q4 actuals and holiday campaign results are available.
