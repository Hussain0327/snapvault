# Q3 Budget Review

Date: July 22, 2026
Attendees: David Chen (Finance), Priya Nair (Engineering), Sofia Reyes (Marketing)

## Purpose

Review actual spend against the Q3 plan and approve adjustments before the quarter closes.

## Decisions

The team decided to cut the Q3 marketing budget from $85,000 to $62,000 after the industry conference was postponed to next year.
Finance approved a $15,000 contractor budget for the data migration project running through the end of September.
The engineering hardware refresh was deferred to Q4 to keep the quarter under the revised total spend cap of $310,000.
Travel spend for the sales team was frozen for the rest of the quarter after exceeding its allotment in June.

## Action Items

- David Chen will present the revised Q3 forecast to leadership on August 28th.
- Sofia Reyes will reallocate the freed marketing budget toward paid search campaigns instead of the conference sponsorship.
- Priya Nair will submit contractor invoices weekly so finance can track the $15,000 budget in real time.

## Discussion

Sofia Reyes raised concerns that cutting the conference sponsorship would reduce lead generation for Q4, but the committee agreed the postponement made the spend moot for this quarter.
David Chen noted that overall department spend is currently 6 percent under plan, mostly due to two open engineering roles that have not yet been filled.
The group agreed to revisit hiring budget in the Q4 review once those roles are closer to being filled.

## Next Meeting

The next budget review is scheduled for October 21st, after Q3 actuals are finalized and the October forecast is drafted.
