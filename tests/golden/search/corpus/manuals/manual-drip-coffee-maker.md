# Drip Coffee Maker Manual - Model DC-220

This manual covers the DC-220 12-cup drip coffee maker, part number DC220-US, including setup, brewing, and cleaning instructions.

## Setup

Fill the water reservoir (part number DC220-TANK) up to the 12-cup line, marked at 1.8 liters.
The unit runs on a standard 120V outlet and draws 900 watts while brewing, noticeably less than a machine with a built-in grinder.

## Filters

Use a size 4 paper filter, or the reusable mesh filter (part number DC220-MESH) included in the box.
Rinse the mesh filter under warm water after every third use to remove oil buildup that can make later batches taste bitter.

## Brewing

Water heats to approximately 96 degrees Celsius during brewing, hotter than most espresso machines since drip coffee needs a longer contact time at a slightly higher temperature.
A full 12-cup pot takes about 9 minutes to brew from start to finish.

## Auto-brew timer

The digital clock can be programmed up to 24 hours in advance; press and hold the PROGRAM button for 3 seconds to set the brew start time.
The timer memory is lost if the machine is unplugged for more than 30 seconds, so it must be reset after any power outage.

## Warming plate

The warming plate keeps coffee at roughly 82 degrees Celsius for up to two hours after brewing, though the flavor noticeably degrades after the first 45 minutes on the plate.

## Cleaning

Run a cleaning cycle with equal parts white vinegar and water every 60 brew cycles, or monthly with typical household use, followed by two cycles of plain water.
A red descale light appears automatically after approximately 80 cycles if the reset button has not been pressed.

## Warranty

The DC-220 includes a 1-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects, shorter than the EC-410 espresso machine's coverage, and does not cover the glass carafe (part number DC220-CARAFE) if it is dropped or cracked.
