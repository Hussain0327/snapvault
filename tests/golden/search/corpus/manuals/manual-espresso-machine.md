# Espresso Machine Manual - Model EC-410

This manual covers setup, operation, and maintenance for the EC-410 home espresso machine, part number EC410-US.

## Setup

Fill the removable water tank (part number EC410-TANK) with fresh, cold water up to the 1.2 liter fill line.
Do not use distilled water, since it lacks the minerals needed to prevent corrosion inside the boiler over time.
Plug into a grounded 120V outlet; the machine draws 1350 watts during heating.

## First use

Before brewing coffee for the first time, run one full tank of plain water through the group head to flush the internal lines.
This takes about four minutes and should be repeated if the machine has not been used for more than two weeks.

## Brewing

The boiler reaches brewing temperature, 93 degrees Celsius, in approximately 90 seconds from a cold start.
Use 18 to 20 grams of finely ground coffee in the double-shot portafilter (part number EC410-PF2) and tamp with about 15 kilograms of pressure for an even extraction.

## Steam wand

Press the steam button and wait for the indicator light to turn solid, which takes roughly 45 seconds after brewing a shot.
The steam wand tip (part number EC410-WAND) should be purged for two seconds before and after frothing to prevent milk residue from clogging the small holes.

## Descaling

Descale every three months with a 1:10 solution of citric acid and water, or sooner if the brew time noticeably lengthens.
Run the descaling solution through both the brew path and steam wand, followed by two full tanks of plain water to rinse.

## Troubleshooting

If the pump makes a loud grinding noise but no water flows, the tank is likely empty or seated incorrectly; reseat it fully until it clicks.
If the machine shuts off mid-brew, check that the drip tray sensor (part number EC410-SENSOR) is not blocked by grounds, since it triggers an automatic shutoff when it thinks the tray is full.

## Warranty

The EC-410 carries a 2-year limited warranty on parts and a 1-year warranty on labor, valid only with proof of purchase from an authorized retailer.
