# Cordless Drill Manual - Model CD-18V

This manual covers the CD-18V cordless drill/driver, part number CD18V-KIT, including battery care, torque settings, and maintenance.

## Battery

The CD-18V ships with two 4.0 amp-hour lithium-ion batteries (part number BAT-18V-4A).
A fully depleted battery reaches full charge in about 55 minutes on the included charger, or 25 minutes using the fast-charge port if the battery temperature is below 40 degrees Celsius.

## Chuck and bits

The keyless chuck accepts bits from 1.5 to 13 millimeters in diameter.
Insert a bit and hand-tighten the chuck collar until it clicks twice, which indicates the internal ratchet has engaged fully.

## Torque settings

The clutch has 20 torque settings plus a dedicated drill setting marked with a drill-bit icon.
Setting 8 is recommended as a starting point for driving standard wood screws into softwood; increase toward setting 15 for hardwood or longer screws.

## Speed settings

A two-speed gearbox switches between 0-450 RPM for high-torque driving and 0-1,650 RPM for drilling.
Switch speeds only when the trigger is fully released, since changing gears under load can strip the gearbox teeth.

## LED work light

The built-in LED light (part number CD18V-LED) activates automatically when the trigger is pressed and stays on for 10 seconds after release.
The light draws negligible battery power, roughly 0.2 percent of a full charge per hour of typical use.

## Maintenance

Clean the chuck jaws monthly with compressed air to remove sawdust buildup, which is the leading cause of bits slipping during use according to the manufacturer's service data.
Store the drill and batteries at room temperature; charging a battery below 0 degrees Celsius can permanently reduce its capacity.

## Warranty

The CD-18V is covered by a 3-year warranty on the tool body and a 2-year warranty on the batteries, provided the original receipt is kept.
