# Space Heater Manual - Model SH-1500

This manual covers safe operation of the SH-1500 ceramic space heater, part number SH1500-US, rated at 1500 watts.

## Placement

Place the heater on a flat, hard surface at least 1 meter from curtains, furniture, and bedding.
Never place the SH-1500 on carpet or a rug, since the manufacturer's testing found that carpeted placement raises the base temperature by an additional 15 degrees Celsius during extended use.

## Power requirements

The SH-1500 draws up to 12.5 amps on the high setting, close to the limit of a standard 15-amp household circuit.
Do not run another high-draw appliance, such as a hair dryer or space heater, on the same circuit at the same time.

## Settings

Three heat settings are available: low (750 watts), high (1500 watts), and fan-only (0 watts of heat output).
The thermostat dial can be set anywhere from 15 to 30 degrees Celsius, and the heater cycles on and off automatically to maintain the target within about 1 degree.

## Safety features

The SH-1500 includes a tip-over switch (part number SH1500-TIP) that cuts power within half a second if the unit is tilted more than 45 degrees from vertical.
An overheat sensor separately shuts the unit off if internal temperature exceeds 95 degrees Celsius, requiring a manual reset by unplugging for 10 minutes.

## Cleaning

Unplug the heater and let it cool for at least 30 minutes before cleaning.
Wipe the exterior with a dry cloth only; do not use water or cleaning sprays near the air intake vents on the back panel.

## Storage

Store the heater in its original box during the off-season, in a dry location away from direct sunlight.
Coil the power cord loosely rather than wrapping it tightly around the base, since tight winding has been linked to internal wire fatigue in the customer service team's repair logs.

## Warranty

The SH-1500 carries a 1-year limited warranty and should be registered online within 30 days of purchase to activate full coverage.
