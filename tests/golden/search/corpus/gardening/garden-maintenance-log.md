# Garden Maintenance Log

A running log of maintenance tasks for the backyard garden beds, kept since the spring cleanup in March.

## March

Mar 14: Turned over all three raised beds and mixed in about 40 liters of compost per bed.
Soil pH tested at 6.4 in the vegetable bed, slightly acidic but within the range tomatoes and peppers prefer.

Mar 28: Applied pre-emergent mulch, 5 centimeters deep, around the perennial border to suppress weeds before the growing season starts in earnest.

## April

Apr 9: First mowing of the season.
Sharpened the mower blade beforehand, which noticeably reduced the ragged tips on the grass compared to last year's first cut.

Apr 22: Noticed aphids on the new pepper seedlings.
Sprayed with a diluted insecticidal soap, 2 tablespoons per liter of water, and will recheck in a week.

## May

May 3: Aphids gone after the second soap application on Apr 29.
Seedlings look recovered with new growth on the top leaves.

May 18: Installed drip irrigation on a timer, running 20 minutes every other morning at 6 AM.
This replaced hand watering, which had become inconsistent on busy weeks.

## June

Jun 6: First harvest of lettuce and radishes.
Lettuce bolted faster than expected once daytime temperatures passed 27 degrees Celsius for three days straight.

Jun 20: Noticed powdery mildew on the squash leaves, likely from the humid week we just had.
Removed the worst-affected leaves and improved airflow by thinning nearby plants.

## Notes

The drip irrigation installed on May 18 cut weekly watering time from about 45 minutes of hand watering to under 5 minutes of checking the timer, the single biggest time savings of the season so far.
